Composition/framing: wide 16:9, centered hero ensemble, ample dark negative space above and around, no cropped devices
Lighting/mood: controlled rim lighting in cyan, amber, and a subtle violet accent; confident, quiet, futuristic
Color palette: near-black, graphite, cyan, amber, restrained violet
Materials/textures: black glass, matte anodized aluminum, soft volumetric glow
Constraints: no people; no third-party logos; no Apple logo; no watermark; no extra devices; do not invent UI panels; retain the product dashboard's overall layout and dark theme; retain the interlocking orbital brand motif accurately
